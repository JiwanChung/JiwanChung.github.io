Bio

I am currently on the job market, for research scientist opportunities in multimodal post training and evaluation + interactive agents.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Artificial Intelligence at Yonsei University, where I also received my Ph.D. in 2026, advised by Professor Seon Joo Kim. Previously, I was a research intern at Microsoft Research, LG AI Research, and Naver Cloud (HyperCLOVA X).

My research focuses on multimodal intelligence, especially reasoning, grounding, and agentic behavior. I develop models and evaluations for systems that reason over visual evidence and make decisions across multiple steps. I have published 16 first-author papers at venues including NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR, ICCV, ACL, and EMNLP.

Research interests: multimodal reasoning, grounded AI, agentic systems, diagnostic evaluation, and representation learning.
